An ambulance in India can take 20 minutes. A brain can’t wait that long.

Survival from cardiac arrest drops by roughly 7–10% for every minute without CPR. The goal is to close the gap between the call and the ambulance with someone who can act.

If someone collapses and isn’t breathing, push. Don’t wait.

  1. Call 112 or 102 and put the phone on speaker.Then press SOS in Golden Minute so a trained person starts walking to you.
  2. Lay the person on their back on a firm surface.Kneel beside the chest, shoulders directly above your hands.
  3. Place the heel of one hand on the centre of the chest, other hand on top.Lock your elbows.
  4. Push hard and fast to the beat, about 5–6 cm deep.Let the chest rise fully each time. Don’t stop until help takes over or an AED tells you to.

Heavy bleeding

Press firmly on the wound with a clean cloth and keep pressing. If blood soaks through, add more cloth on top; don’t lift it. Raise the limb if you can.
